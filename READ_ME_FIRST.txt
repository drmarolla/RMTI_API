SHOW THE CASE-STUDY ANIMATION ON THE HOME PAGE
1. Unzip. GitHub > Add file > Upload files. Drag in: Home.py, READ_ME_FIRST.txt, the pages folder and the animation folder. Commit.
2. Open https://oncoclastics.streamlit.app/Home  (hard refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R). The animation appears there right away.
3. To make it the FIRST page at https://oncoclastics.streamlit.app :
   share.streamlit.io > your app > three dots > Settings > Main file path: change app.py.py to Home.py > Save.
   Then Manage app > Reboot app.
